# F1.12 Area objects and signposts

Status: built ([#126](https://github.com/APRS-NET/AprsMap/issues/126)). Area objects draw as circle, ellipse, box,
triangle or line (with its corridor as a band), filled or outlined, under tracks and symbols, fading with the object;
APRS's 16 colours fold onto `Color.Area1`…`Color.Area8` in every palette (3:1 against the ground, tested). Signposts
show their text on a small sign. Settings › Map › Show area shapes, on by default. Decision 1 as proposed. Anchoring,
from APRS101 ch. 11: circle and ellipse centred on the position, the box hanging down and right from it, the triangle's
apex at it, lines down and right (type 1) or down and left (type 6). The corridor's `{nnn}` is drawn as the band's
full width.

## User story

As an operator at an event, I see the areas that net control marks on the map (a race course corridor, a search
sector, a closed road) as shapes rather than as a single symbol, and highway signposts show their number, so the map
matches what people on the ground are told.

## Behavior

### Area objects

1. An object or item with the **area symbol** (`\l`) and a shape extension is drawn as its shape (APRS.NET
   `AreaShape`): circle, ellipse, triangle, box, or line with a corridor width. Sizes come from APRS 1.1's corrected
   formula (`AreaShape.LatitudeOffsetDegrees` and `LongitudeOffsetDegrees`).
2. Filled shapes (types 5–9, except the line) are drawn with a translucent fill; others as an outline. Lines with a
   corridor width draw the corridor as a translucent band.
3. **Colours**: APRS's 16 area colours map to 8 palette tokens (`Color.Area.1`…`Color.Area.8`, bright and dark
   variants sharing a token), defined in every palette so they stay distinguishable in high-contrast and
   colour-vision-safe palettes (F0.6, checked by the palette tests). Fills use the palette's area fill opacity.
4. Shapes are drawn under tracks and symbols, and the object's symbol and label still show at its position.
5. Shapes follow the object's life: they move, fade (F1.3 stale) and disappear (expired or killed) with it.

### Signposts

6. An object or item with the **signpost symbol** (`\m`) and a `{…}` value in its comment (APRS.NET
   `PositionFields.Signpost`) is drawn as a small sign with that value (up to 3 characters, such as a speed limit or
   a route number), in the theme's text and surface colours.

### Settings

7. **Area shapes** (Settings › Map, and a map layer toggle): On · Off. Default On.

## Service surface

- `StationState` (the map stream) gains `Area` (kind, filled, colour index 0–15, extents in degrees, corridor width)
  and `Signpost` (text), only for objects and items that have them.

## APRS.NET dependencies

- `AreaShape` and `Signpost` decoding: delivered. No new gap.

## Desktop vs web differences

None.

## Acceptance tests

1. Each shape type 0–9 from APRS101 ch. 11 examples draws the expected shape and fill; a line with `{015}` draws a
   15-mile corridor.
2. All 16 colours map to tokens that pass the palette distinctness check in every palette.
3. A killed area object removes its shape; a moved one moves it.
4. A signpost shows its value; Area shapes Off hides shapes but not symbols.

## Decisions for review

1. **16 APRS colours onto 8 tokens** (proposed): the bright and dark variants of a hue look alike to many viewers
   anyway, and 8 hues can be kept distinct in every palette.
