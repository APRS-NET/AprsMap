# F2.6 Email and QRU

Status: built for 0.6.0 ([#201](https://github.com/APRS-NET/AprsMap/issues/201)) except the QRU object server (later,
behavior 9); decisions proposed 2026-10-19 for the maintainer's review. In the UI: Messages › Email and QRU.

## User story

As an operator away from the Internet, I send a short email through the APRS email gateway without remembering its
address format, and I ask the QRU servers around me what they offer (repeaters, nets, local information) and get it on
my map.

## Background: what already exists

- Messaging (F2.4): conversations with acks and retries, routes, and every send following F2.1.
- The APRS-IS email gateway (aprs-is.net, Email Services): a message to `EMAIL-2` whose text is an email address, or a
  shortcut the sender set up, then the email's text, 67 characters in all. It must be a numbered message with an ack;
  the gateway's notices (accepted, delayed, failed) come back as messages from `EMAIL-2`. Shortcuts are made, listed
  and removed by messages to `EMAIL-2` too.
- QRU servers (YAAC's documentation, Querying Stations): a message to `QRU` whose text is a
  group code asks every QRU server in range for what it holds in that group; `INFO` asks each to say which groups it
  serves and how far. Servers answer with objects and items, sent once, and with messages.
- Observed in APRSIS32 (docs/reference/functional-inventory.md): email via the gateway; a QRU-style object server.
  This design is the app's own.

## Behavior

### Email

1. **Email** in Messages (proposed: under the conversation list, beside New message): **To** (an email address or a
   shortcut) and the **text**. **Send email** sends `To text` to `EMAIL-2` as an ordinary message, acked and retried,
   and opens the `EMAIL-2` conversation, where the gateway's notices arrive.
2. **One message only** (proposed): `To`, a space and the text together are at most 67 characters, counted as typed;
   a longer one isn't split (the gateway would take each part as a new email) but refused, saying how much too long.
3. `To` is one word: an address with `@`, or a shortcut of letters and digits. Shortcut commands (`name address`,
   `name L`, `name R`) are ordinary messages to `EMAIL-2`, sent from its conversation (proposed: no form of their own).

### QRU

4. **Ask QRU servers**: a group code, `INFO` by default, sent to `QRU` once on every source that can send now,
   **without a message ID** (proposed): it's a question to whoever is in range, so nothing acks or retries it.
5. The group code is 1 to 9 characters, letters, digits and `-` (proposed), upper-cased.
6. Answers need nothing new: objects and items go on the map (F1.3), and messages from servers open conversations (F2.4).
7. Every F2.1 rule applies: Transmit on, a licensed profile, sources not RX only.
8. **What was asked**, and on how many sources, shows under the button ("Asked QRU servers for INFO on 2 sources").

### Later

9. **Being a QRU server** (answering queries with this station's objects, by group, within a range, at most every so
   often) waits for a follow-up (proposed): it needs groups on F2.7's objects and limits worth their own spec.
10. SMS gateways (SMSGTE and the like) aren't covered (proposed): they're separate services with their own rules.

## Service surface

- Email: none new; the UI sends with `SendMessageAsync("EMAIL-2", …)`.
- `IEngineService.AskQruAsync(string group)`: sends the query; returns the copies sent. Errors as Send position now,
  plus `message.qru-group-invalid`.

## APRS.NET dependencies

G10 (messaging, delivered): `MessagePayload` without an ID.

## Acceptance tests

1. Email to `n0call@example.com` with "Running late" sends `n0call@example.com Running late` to `EMAIL-2`, acked, and
   opens that conversation; 68 characters in all are refused with how much too long; a `To` with a space is refused.
2. Ask QRU servers sends `:QRU      :INFO` without an ID on each source that can send, and says so; a bad group is
   refused; with Transmit off nothing goes and the reason shows.
3. A browser that isn't a signed-in operator can't ask.

## Decisions for review

All proposed (2026-10-19), built as described so they can be tried:

1. **Email and QRU in Messages**, under the conversation list.
2. **An email is one message**: over 67 characters is refused, not split.
3. **Shortcut commands through the `EMAIL-2` conversation**, no form of their own.
4. **QRU queries go once, without an ID**, on every source that can send.
5. **Group codes**: 1 to 9 letters, digits and `-`, `INFO` by default.
6. **Being a QRU server waits** for its own spec, after objects get groups.
7. **No SMS gateway helper.**
