# F1.10 Weather stations

Status: built ([#124](https://github.com/APRS-NET/AprsMap/issues/124)) except behavior 7, which comes with the view
filters (F1.9), and raw weather formats, which APRS.NET doesn't decode (G18). Built: readings with their own times from
weather reports and positions with weather; the Weather section in the details with each reading's age; the map
label's weather line (Settings › Map › Weather labels); wind barbs (Settings › Map › Wind, off by default); and
Settings › Units for the station's units. Decisions 1 and 2 as proposed. A weather station's course and speed are its
wind, so they no longer show as its motion.

## User story

As an operator, I can see what the weather stations around me report: temperature, wind, rain, pressure and humidity,
in my units, on the map and in the station's details, and I can tell at a glance where the wind is strong or the
temperature low.

## Behavior

### Readings

1. The store (F1.3) keeps the **latest weather readings** for each station, object or item that sends them: from
   weather reports (`_`), positions with weather (`WeatherData` on a position), and raw weather formats APRS.NET
   decodes. Each reading keeps its own time, because a station may send wind and rain in different packets.
2. Readings: wind direction, speed and gust; temperature; rain in the last hour, last 24 hours and since midnight;
   humidity; pressure; luminosity; snow. A reading the packet doesn't carry keeps its last value; a reading older than
   the station's stale time (F1.3, 30 min) is shown as stale.

### Display

3. **Details pane** (F1.6): a Weather section with each known reading, its age when older than the station's last
   packet, and a wind compass (direction as an arrow and compass point).
4. **Map label** (Settings › Map › Weather labels: Off · Temperature · Temperature and wind; default Temperature): a
   second line under the callsign of weather stations, for example `14 °C · NW 22 km/h`.
5. **Wind barbs** (moved here from F1.4, its decision 2; Settings › Map › Wind: Off · Barbs; default Off): a barb at the
   station pointing from where the wind blows, with flags for speed (standard 5 / 10 / 50 knot steps), drawn in the
   theme's text colour with the label halo.
6. **Units** follow the unit settings that already exist (F0.4, `UnitSettings`: temperature, wind, rain, pressure,
   altitude, distance). This feature adds their Settings › Units section to the settings page (F0.6 registry).
7. Weather stations are a view filter condition (F1.9, "with weather").

## Service surface

- `StationDetail` gains `Weather` (readings, each with value and time, in fixed engine units: °C, m/s, mm, hPa); the
  UI converts to the chosen units with APRS.NET's conversions (G08).
- `StationState` (the map stream) gains a compact `WeatherSummary` (temperature, wind direction and speed) for labels
  and barbs, only for stations with weather.
- No new settings: `AppSettings.Units` (F0.4) already has a unit per quantity.

## APRS.NET dependencies

- Weather decoding (`WeatherReport`, `WeatherData`, `RawWeatherReport`): delivered.
- **G08, unit conversions**: delivered (`0.2.0-alpha.2`, `AprsUnits.Convert` for length, speed, temperature and
  pressure; rain is a length).

## Desktop vs web differences

None.

## Acceptance tests

1. A position with weather, then a weather report with only rain: both readings kept with their own times.
2. Each unit choice formats each reading in every language (pseudo-language check).
3. Labels and barbs draw for weather stations only; barbs point from the wind's direction with the right flags for 5,
   15, 55 and 65 knots.
4. A stale reading shows its age.

## Decisions for review

1. **Wind barbs move here from F1.4** (as F1.4's decision 2 proposes) and are off by default.
2. **Engine units are metric**, converted in the UI (proposed), so the contract has one form.
