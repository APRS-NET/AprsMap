# F2.7 Objects and items

Status: built for 0.6.0 ([#199](https://github.com/APRS-NET/AprsMap/issues/199)) except multiline objects (gap G36);
decisions proposed 2026-10-19 for the maintainer's review. In the UI: Settings › Objects; the map's right-click menu.

## User story

As an operator, I put things on the map for others: a net's meeting point, a repeater, an event's aid station. I place
one where I right-click, give it a name, a symbol and a comment, move it when it moves, and kill it when it's done. It
goes out soon after each change and then less and less often, and everyone sees it as mine.

## Background: what already exists

- APRS.NET builds objects (`;`, with a timestamp) and items (`)`, without), live or killed (G01), and decaying beacon
  schedules (G11, `DecayBeaconSchedule`), which F2.2's position beacon uses.
- Received objects and items are stations on the map (F1.3), killed ones removed.
- What this station sends is listed as Sent and applied to the map like anything heard (F2.1 behavior 13).
- Observed in APRSIS32 (docs/reference/observation-2026-10-13.md, docs/reference/functional-inventory.md): objects and
  items placed from the map, moved, killed, re-beaconed; multiline objects. This design is the app's own.

## Behavior

### Placing and changing

1. **Place an object here**, on the map's right-click menu, opens Settings › Objects with a new object at that spot.
   Settings › Objects › **Add** starts one without a spot (coordinates typed).
2. **What one has**:
   1. **Object or item** (proposed default: object). An object carries the time it's sent; an item doesn't, for
      things that don't change (APRS101 chapter 11).
   2. **Name**: an object 1 to 9 characters, an item 3 to 9 without `!` or `_`; printable ASCII, no space at either
      end, unique in the profile (proposed: case matters, as on the air).
   3. **Location**, typed as coordinates in any form the setup wizard reads.
   4. **Symbol**, from the same list as the station's; red X (`/.`) by default (proposed). The emergency symbol isn't
      offered (the app sends no emergency, F2.12 decision 3).
   5. **Comment**, up to 43 characters (APRS101 chapter 11).
   6. **Interval**, the longest wait between sends, 10 minutes to 24 hours, 30 minutes by default (proposed).
   7. **Send via**, the profile's sources that may send; all by default, as for beacons.
3. **Save and send** stores it in the profile and sends it soon (behavior 5). Changing anything, its place included,
   starts its schedule again.
4. **Move an object here**, on the right-click menu, lists the profile's live objects; choosing one moves it to that
   spot, as editing its coordinates would.

### Sending

5. **Decay** (proposed): each object goes out on each of its sources at once, then after 30 seconds on APRS-IS or 2
   minutes on a TNC, doubling each time up to its interval, then every interval. The 10 seconds between any two
   beacons on a source (F2.2 behavior 6) holds for objects too.
6. Every F2.1 rule applies, as for beacons: nothing goes out while Transmit is off, the profile may not transmit, a
   source is RX only or switched off, or the station's position isn't yet confirmed; objects wait and go when they can.
7. **Kept**: objects belong to the profile and are kept with its settings (proposed), so they carry on after a restart
   until killed.

### Killing

8. **Kill** sends the object killed, three times on the decay schedule (proposed), then removes it from the list.
   Until then the list shows it as being killed.
9. **Forget** removes one without sending anything: for one never sent, or when the kill can't go out.

### Seen

10. Objects go on the map from what's sent, like anything heard, so they show where others see them, with this
    station as their owner (F1.3).

### Not yet

11. **Multiline objects** (lines and areas drawn from an object) wait for gap G36: the format is specified outside
    APRS101 and isn't in APRS.NET yet.

## Service surface

- `OwnObject(Id, Name, Kind, Latitude, Longitude, Symbol, Comment, IntervalMinutes, SourceIds, Live)` with
  `OwnObjectKind` (`Object`, `Item`); `StationProfile.Objects`.
- `IEngineService.PlaceObjectAsync(OwnObject)`: adds or changes one in the active profile and starts its schedule.
- `IEngineService.RemoveObjectAsync(string id, bool kill)`: kills it (sent killed, then removed) or forgets it.
- Saving settings keeps the stored objects; only these two calls change them, so a stale save can't bring one back.
- Error codes: `settings.object.name-invalid`, `settings.object.name-taken`, `settings.object.invalid`,
  `settings.object.unknown`.

## APRS.NET dependencies

G01 (object and item builders, delivered), G11 (decay schedule, delivered); G36 for multiline objects (not filed).

## Acceptance tests

1. Placing an object sends `;NAME     *DDHHMMzLAT/LON$comment` on each source at once; an item `)NAME!LAT/LON$comment`.
2. Its re-sends follow 30 s, 1, 2, 4… minutes on APRS-IS up to the interval.
3. Moving it sends the new place at once and starts the schedule again.
4. Kill sends it killed three times, then it's gone from the profile; Forget sends nothing.
5. A bad or duplicate name, a long comment, a bad place or interval, and the emergency symbol are refused.
6. Saving settings with an old copy of the profile doesn't bring back a killed object.
7. A browser that isn't a signed-in operator can't place, move or kill.
8. Right-click › Place an object here opens the form at that spot; Move an object here moves it.

## Decisions for review

All proposed (2026-10-19), built as described so they can be tried:

1. **Settings › Objects** is where they're listed and edited; the map menu places and moves.
2. **Kept per profile with its settings**, beaconing after a restart.
3. **Decay from 30 s (APRS-IS) or 2 min (TNC)**, doubling to the interval (30 minutes by default).
4. **Killed: sent three times, then removed**; Forget removes without sending.
5. **Red X by default**; names are case-sensitive and unique in the profile.
6. **Multiline objects wait** for gap G36.
