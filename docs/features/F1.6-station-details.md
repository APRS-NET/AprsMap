# F1.6 Station details

Status: building ([#68](https://github.com/APRS-NET/AprsMap/issues/68))

Built: the service surface. `StationDetail` carries the locator, the device, how the last packet arrived and the last
packet's raw text, worked out from the stored bytes when asked for. Still to build: the Details panel.

## User story

As an operator, when I select a station I see everything known about it in one place: what and where it is, what it
last said, how it reached me, how far away it is and what equipment sent it. From there I can follow it, centre on
it, clear its track or (later) message it.

## Behavior

### The pane

1. Selecting a station on the map (F1.4), in the station list (F1.7) or in the packet list (F1.8) opens the **Details
   panel** (F1.0; docked on the right by default, and movable like any panel), or a bottom sheet on a narrow window
   (UX doc, "Shell"). Escape or Close closes
   it and clears the selection.
2. It shows, from `GetStationAsync` (F1.3), each line only when known:
   1. **Heading**: symbol, name, kind (station, object or item, with the owner for objects and items, and the
      previous owner when it changed);
   2. **Last heard**: relative ("4 min ago") with the time in a tooltip, and the packet count;
   3. **Position**: latitude and longitude in the viewer's coordinate format, the locator (6 characters), the
      ambiguity when there is one, and **distance and bearing from me** (F1.13);
   4. **Motion**: speed, course and altitude, in the viewer's units;
   5. **Comment** and **status** text, as sent (control characters shown as symbols, never interpreted);
   6. **How it arrived** (G20): heard directly on RF, via digipeaters (named), or from APRS-IS through an IGate (named),
      and the full path on request;
   7. **Device** (G07): vendor and model from the destination (TOCALL) or Mic-E code, for example "Yaesu FTM-500D";
   8. **Last packet**: the raw text, selectable and copyable.
3. The pane **updates live** while open: a new packet from the station refreshes it; a removed station shows "No
   longer heard" and keeps the last details until closed.

### Actions

4. **Centre**: centre the map on it. **Follow**: keep the map centred on it as it moves (F1.7 behavior 6).
   **Clear track** (F1.5). **Copy position** (coordinates in the viewer's format). **Send message** appears with
   messaging (F2.6).
5. Each action is a button with an accessible name; the pane is reachable and usable with the keyboard and a screen
   reader (it is the accessible view of a station, with the station list).

### Formatting

6. Numbers, times and units follow the viewer's culture and units (F0.4, F0.9). Coordinates use the viewer's format
   (decimal degrees, degrees-minutes, degrees-minutes-seconds; Settings › Units).

## Service surface

- `GetStationAsync` (F1.3) as the source; `StationDetail` gains `DistanceKm` and `BearingDegrees` (F1.13), `Locator`
  (6 characters), `Device` (`StationDevice`: vendor, model, class name; G07), `Path` (the raw path), `Arrival`
  (`StationArrival`: direct, digipeated with the digipeaters' names, internet, or unknown, and the IGate or server;
  G20; a third-party packet arrived by the route inside), and `LastPacket` (the raw text as received). The store keeps
  only the last packet's bytes and works these out when the details are asked for.
- Selection is UI state, not an engine member; the details update from the station stream's change for that name
  (F0.2), then a fresh `GetStationAsync`.

## APRS.NET dependencies

- **G07, device identification**, **G08, geodesy** (distance and bearing, via F1.13) and **G20, path analysis**:
  delivered (`0.2.0-alpha.2`: `AprsNet.DeviceId`, `Geodesy`, `PathAnalysis`).

## Acceptance tests

1. Selecting a station shows each known line and hides unknown ones; objects show owner and previous owner.
2. A new packet refreshes the open pane; a removed station shows No longer heard.
3. Every line formats in every language including the pseudo-language, and in imperial and metric units.
4. The pane is keyboard-navigable; Escape closes it and clears the selection; the 200 % render check (F0.6) shows no
   clipping in every palette.
5. After G07, G08, G20: the device, distance and bearing, and arrival lines appear from corpus packets.
6. Control characters in a comment are shown as symbols.
