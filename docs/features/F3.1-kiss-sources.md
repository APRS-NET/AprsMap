# F3.1 KISS sources (receive)

Status: KISS over TCP built ([#120](https://github.com/APRS-NET/AprsMap/issues/120)) for 0.4.0-alpha.1; KISS over
serial in 0.5.0-alpha.1 ([#177](https://github.com/APRS-NET/AprsMap/issues/177)). Receive only:
transmitting through a TNC is Phase 2 (F2.x) and needs a verified login and, on a gateway, an operator. Built: KISS over
TCP (behaviors 1.1, 2, 4, 5, 7): Settings › Sources adds one with its host, port (8001) and TNC port (0), and the
profiles that use it (a new one is ticked for the active profile); the engine opens it with APRS.NET's
`KissTcpConnection`, which reconnects by itself, and its pill says connected, or reconnecting with why (closed,
unreachable, link failed) and the wait; frames that aren't APRS are dropped; packets carry the source's id and go
through the same duplicate check, store and lists. Tested live against Direwolf. "Heard by this station" (8): a
packet from a TNC is heard directly when no digipeater marked itself, or through the digipeaters that did; the details
and the route say "this station" and the route ends at its location, the packet list's markers say so, and the
network's last link ends at this station's callsign (decision 2, as a `HeardHere` flag on the arrival, the route and
the packet's markers rather than a new arrival kind, so "heard directly" in the View filter covers both). Each TNC's
pill is made like the APRS-IS one (5, 6): coloured by its
state (the OK colours when connected, the warning colour when it can't reconnect), a switch inside on its left that
turns it off and on for the station (saved with the toggles, `SetSourceEnabledAsync`; the Internet switch doesn't touch
it, decision 3), "Direwolf off" while off, and a menu with Reconnect now (`ReconnectSourceAsync`), both with the
settings gate on a gateway. This station (the active profile's callsign) can be selected on the map by its house:
its details show its location and locator, and the stations its own radio heard in the last hour, directly and through
digipeaters, nearest first, each a link; its RF links draw from the profile's location until it's heard itself
(maintainer request, 2026-10-16). The packet list's **Source** choice (10), shown when the active profile uses more
than one source, lists packets that arrived on that source or were also seen on it (`PacketFilter.SourceId`); a list
showing one source gets a packet as soon as it's heard there, even when another source was first. The dashboard's
Connection rows (F1.22) carry each TNC's switch, the same one as in its pill, and say "Off" for a source switched off.
The wizard's server step has **Use a TNC instead** (3): the KISS over TCP host and port (8001), an existing source that
connects there reused, no APRS-IS source, and no passcode step, so it's three steps (an edited profile keeps its
passcode for when APRS-IS is added back). It offers KISS over TCP only until serial is built. KISS over serial (1.2) moved to
0.5.0-alpha.1 ([#177](https://github.com/APRS-NET/AprsMap/issues/177)) to be tested on the hardware.

## User story

As an operator with a radio, I connect the app to my TNC (Direwolf or soundmodem over the network, or a hardware TNC
such as an MFJ TNC-X or a NinoTNC on a USB serial port) and see what my station hears on RF, beside or instead of
APRS-IS, with each packet saying where it came from and stations heard on both counted once.

## Behavior

### Adding a TNC

1. **Settings › Sources › Add a source** offers the two KISS kinds that F1.25 lists as "coming":
   1. **KISS over TCP**: host and port (default **8001**, Direwolf's and soundmodem's KISS port), and a name
      defaulting to the host;
   2. **KISS over serial**: the port, picked from the serial ports present (with a way to type one that isn't listed
      yet), the speed (default **9600**), and a name defaulting to the port.
   Both have the TNC **port** (0–15, default 0) for multi-port TNCs, under "More".
2. A profile (F0.8) ticks any number of KISS sources, with or without its APRS-IS source (F1.25 behaviors 3, 4). A
   profile with only KISS sources is a **receive-only RF station**: no APRS-IS login, nothing sent anywhere.
3. **The setup wizard** (F0.5) gains **Use a TNC instead** on its server step (F1.25 behavior 7): it asks the KISS
   kind and its details, and creates the source for the profile, with no APRS-IS source.

### Connecting

4. The engine opens a profile's KISS sources beside its APRS-IS session (F1.25 behaviors 8–10, already built for the
   source list). Each reconnects on its own with a growing wait after the TNC drops or the port disappears: a serial
   TNC unplugged and plugged back in comes back without a restart.
5. Each KISS source has its own **status pill** (F1.25 behavior 11): connecting, connected, or reconnecting with the
   reason and the countdown, worded as the APRS-IS pill is. Its menu offers **Reconnect now** for that source.
6. The quick toggles (F1.15) gain the KISS sources: each can be turned off and on. The Internet switch doesn't affect
   them: a TCP TNC on this machine or the local network isn't the internet (decision 3).

### Receiving

7. Each KISS frame carries an AX.25 UI frame; the engine reads it into an APRS packet the same way APRS-IS lines are
   read, records its source (F1.25 behavior 8) and passes it through the same duplicate check, store, map and lists.
   Frames that aren't APRS (other protocols on the channel) are counted and dropped.
8. **Heard by me**: a packet from a KISS source was heard on RF by this station. Its route (F1.21) ends at this
   station rather than an IGate: heard directly when no digipeater is marked as having repeated it, or through the
   digipeaters that did. The details say "Heard directly by this station" or "Heard by this station through N0DIGI-1".
9. A packet heard both on a TNC and on APRS-IS keeps the first and adds the other to **Seen on** (F1.25 behavior 9).
10. The packet list (F1.8) can be filtered **by source** (F1.25's leftover), so "only what my TNC heard" is one choice.
11. Nothing received on a TNC is sent anywhere: no IGate (F3.4) and no digipeating (F3.5) in this feature.

### On a gateway

12. A serial TNC plugs into the gateway's host; a container needs the device passed through (`--device`). A TCP TNC is
    reached from the gateway, so `localhost` means the gateway's host. Adding or changing a source has the settings
    gate (F0.4), as for APRS-IS sources. The deployment notes say how to pass a USB serial device into the container.

## Service surface

- `SourceSettings` gains the KISS details: `Host` and `Port` (TCP), `SerialPort` and `Baud` (serial), `TncPort`
  (0–15). `Server` and `Filter` stay APRS-IS only.
- New `Task<IReadOnlyList<string>> GetSerialPortsAsync()`: the serial ports present on the engine's machine (behavior
  1.2); on a gateway, the gateway's, behind the settings gate.
- New `Task ReconnectSourceAsync(string sourceId)` (behavior 5), with the same gate as `ReconnectAsync`.
- `SourceStatus` (F1.25 behavior 11) is unchanged: KISS connections report the same states.
- `StationArrival` and `PacketRoute` (F1.21) gain "heard by this station": an `ArrivalKind` for the station's own
  receiver, with the gateway hop left out.
- `PacketFilter` (F1.8) gains `SourceId`.

## APRS.NET dependencies

- **G12, AX.25 UI frames**: delivered (`0.2.0-alpha.3`, `AprsNet.Ax25`: `Ax25Frame.ToAprsPacket`).
- **G13, KISS over TCP**: delivered (`0.2.0-alpha.3`, `AprsNet.Kiss`: `KissTcpConnection` with `KissTcpOptions`, its
  `StateChanged` with the reconnect reason and wait, and its metrics).
- **G13, KISS over serial** that reopens after an unplug, with port listing: published in `0.3.0-alpha.2`
  (`AprsNet.Kiss.Serial`: `KissSerialConnection` with `KissSerialOptions`, and `GetPortNames()`), with a dead-link
  fix for both transports in `0.3.0-alpha.3` (`KissTcpOptions.DeadLinkTimeout`).
  [APRS.NET#126](https://github.com/APRS-NET/APRS.NET/issues/126) stays open until it's tested on hardware.

## Acceptance tests

1. A fake KISS TCP server (a test double sending KISS frames) gives packets that reach the store with the source's id;
   a frame that isn't APRS is dropped.
2. The TCP connection drops and reconnects with the pill showing Reconnecting and the wait; Reconnect now connects at
   once.
3. A packet heard on the TNC and on APRS-IS within 30 s shows once, with Seen on naming the other (F1.25 acceptance).
4. A KISS packet heard directly shows "Heard directly by this station" and a route ending at this station.
5. Serial (with APRS.NET#126): a fake port that disappears and returns reconnects without a restart; the port list
   shows the ports present.
6. A profile with only a KISS source never logs in to APRS-IS.
7. Manual, on the test bed: Direwolf on Windows over TCP, an MFJ TNC-X on USB serial, later a NinoTNC.

## Decisions for review

1. **KISS over TCP first** (proposed): it's buildable now on APRS.NET 0.2.0-alpha.3; serial follows APRS.NET#126 in the
   same milestone.
2. **"Heard by this station" as its own arrival kind** (proposed), rather than treating the station as an IGate, so
   nothing suggests it gated anything.
3. **The Internet switch doesn't stop KISS sources** (proposed): a network TNC is local equipment.
4. **Defaults**: TCP port 8001, serial 9600 baud, TNC port 0 (proposed).
