# F3.4 Receive-only IGate

Status: built for 0.6.0 ([#206](https://github.com/APRS-NET/AprsMap/issues/206)); decisions proposed 2026-10-21 for the
maintainer's review. Two-way gating (messages from APRS-IS to RF) is [F3.4-two-way-igate](F3.4-two-way-igate.md), 0.7.0
([#208](https://github.com/APRS-NET/AprsMap/issues/208)). In the UI: Settings › Sources › IGate.

## User story

As an operator with a TNC and an internet connection, I pass what my radio hears to APRS-IS, so stations around me
show on everyone's maps, following the IGate rules so I don't flood or loop the network.

## Background: what already exists

- Sources (F1.25): a profile's APRS-IS source and its TNCs (KISS over TCP or serial).
- Transmit (F2.1): the Transmit switch, a licensed profile and a verified APRS-IS login before anything is sent.
- APRS.NET 0.5.0-alpha.1's `AprsNet.IGate` (G16): `RfToInternetGate` decides which packets heard on RF go to APRS-IS
  under the aprs-is.net IGate rules (not from the internet, not `NOGATE` or `RFONLY`, not general queries, not
  already gated, not too long, each once in 30 s) and builds the line, adding `qAO` and the IGate's callsign.

## Behavior

1. **Gate what my TNCs hear to APRS-IS** is a switch in Settings › Sources, for the active profile, **off by default**
   (proposed): gating puts the station's name on other people's packets, so it's something to choose.
2. **What's gated**: every packet the profile's own receivers hear (its TNCs, not APRS-IS), under APRS.NET's rules.
   Packets from APRS-IS are never considered.
3. **Held** (proposed): nothing is gated without an APRS-IS source, with Transmit off, with a profile that may not send
   or a login that isn't verified: the same rules as anything else the station sends (F2.1). The card says why it's
   held, in the same words as the Transmit pill.
4. **Receive only**: gated packets carry `qAO`, which tells APRS-IS the IGate can't pass messages back to RF. The help
   text says that receive-only IGates are discouraged on the shared APRS frequency, since nearby stations may take
   them for a way to be reached.
5. Packets gated aren't listed again: the packet list already shows them as heard on the TNC.
6. **Counts**: the card says how many packets were gated and how many the rules kept back since the app started;
   each refusal is logged at debug level with its reason, and each gated line too (F1.32, Transmit area).

## Service surface

- `StationProfile.IGate` (`bool?`, null for off; `EffectiveIGate`).
- `EngineStatus.IGate` (`IGateStatus`: gated, refused, and the error it's held by), null when the profile isn't an
  IGate.
- No new `IEngineService` member: the switch saves the profile.

## APRS.NET dependencies

G16 (`AprsNet.IGate`, delivered in 0.5.0-alpha.1).

## Acceptance tests

1. A packet heard on the TNC goes to APRS-IS with `,qAO,` and the profile's callsign after its path; a digipeated copy
   isn't gated again and is counted as not.
2. Packets from the internet (`TCPIP`), with `NOGATE`, and general queries aren't gated; packets from APRS-IS aren't
   considered.
3. With Transmit off or a login that isn't verified, nothing is gated and the status says why.
4. Off by default: nothing is gated and the status has no IGate.
5. Settings › Sources shows the card; the switch saves the active profile; the counts and hold follow the status.

## Decisions for review

All proposed (2026-10-21), built as described so they can be tried:

1. **Off by default**, per profile.
2. **Held by every transmit rule**: Transmit on, a licensed profile, a verified login.
3. **All the profile's own receivers** are gated; no per-TNC choice yet.
4. **Receive only** (`qAO`), with the caution about the shared frequency in the help text.
5. **Counts since the app started** on the card, not kept across restarts.
6. **In Settings › Sources**, beside the sources it joins, rather than in Beacons or Messaging.
