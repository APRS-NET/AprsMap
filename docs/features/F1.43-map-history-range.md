# F1.43 Stations heard in the last days, on the map

Status: built for 0.8.0 ([#455](https://github.com/APRS-NET/AprsMap/issues/455), maintainer request, 2026-10-27);
decision 1 by the maintainer, the rest proposed, for review before it ships. Builds on the packet history (F1.41).

## User story

After a net, a storm or a weekend event, I want to see who was on the air, not only who was heard in the last two
hours. I choose **Also show stations heard in the last 3 days**, and the map adds, dimmed, every station heard in that
time in the area I'm looking at, at its last position. I select one to see what the history has: when it was last
heard, its last packet, its track over the days and its kept packets. I turn it off and the map is live only again.

## Background: what already exists

- The map, the station list and Details show the stations the store holds (F1.3): those heard within `RemoveAfter`,
  2 hours by default. Stale ones are faded (F1.3 behavior 7).
- The packet history (F1.41) keeps every packet for up to 30 days, findable by station, time and position, and gives a
  station's longer track and kept packets in Details (F1.41 behaviors 8, 9).
- The View filter (F1.9) is per viewer, kept across sessions, and its chip says when it hides stations.

## Behavior

1. **The choice**, in the map's Filter panel, under the conditions: **Also show stations heard in the last: Off ·
   24 hours · 3 days · 7 days** (Off by default; up to the history's length). Per viewer, kept with the View filter
   (F1.9 behavior 7). It adds stations and hides none, so it applies whether My filter or All is on.
2. **What's added**: every station, object and item the history has a position for in that time, in the area the map
   shows, that the store doesn't hold now, at its last kept position with its last symbol. Drawn faded like a stale
   station (F1.3 behavior 7), labelled as stations are, with no track unless selected.
3. **Bounded**: at most 2,000 added at once, the most recently heard first; the chip says how many came from the
   history, and when the limit was reached ("+2,000 from the last 7 days, the most recent"). Loaded again when the map
   stops moving (half a second after), and every minute while it's on.
4. **Selecting one** shows it in Details as the history has it: the details its kept packets give (comment, status,
   path, device, position, last packet), "Last heard 2 days ago", and F1.41's longer track and kept packets. Follow and
   Centre work; Send message works as for any callsign.
5. **Read-only**: added stations never count on the dashboard, never alert, and never change the station store. A
   station heard again joins the store and is drawn live as usual.
6. **Without a history** (off, or a desktop without a data folder), the choice isn't offered.
7. **On a gateway**, a viewer the home's location is hidden from (F1.26 behavior 11) doesn't get the home station from
   the history.

## Service surface

- `IEngineService.GetKeptStationsAsync(KeptStationsQuery(Since, South, West, North, East, Max))` →
  `KeptStations(Stations, Total)`: each station's last kept position and symbol, as `StationState`s marked stale, most
  recent first; `Total` before the limit.
- `IEngineService.GetKeptStationAsync(string name)` → `StationDetail?`: the station as its last kept packets describe
  it, worked out by applying them to a store of their own; null when the history has none.

## APRS.NET dependencies

None.

## Desktop vs web differences

None; the browser and the desktop as a gateway's client ask the gateway, which has the history.

## Acceptance tests

1. With 3 days chosen, a station heard yesterday in the map's area is drawn faded at its last position; one heard 5
   days ago isn't; one outside the area isn't.
2. A station both in the store and in the history is drawn once, live.
3. More than the limit: the most recent are drawn, and the chip says the limit was reached.
4. Selecting an added station shows its kept details, track and packets.
5. A viewer shielded from the home doesn't get the home station from the history.

## Decisions for review

1. **"Heard since", not a time slider** (maintainer, 2026-10-27, #455: the scope was to be outlined first): the map
   shows who was heard, at their last positions. A slider replaying positions at a moment ("the map at 14:00
   Saturday") can follow.
2. **Only the selected station's track** (proposed), via F1.41's longer track, not every added station's: many
   tracks over 7 days would be heavy on a phone.
3. **The station list doesn't follow it yet** (proposed): the list shows the store, as now; listing the added stations,
   marked "from history", can follow once the map's version is tried.
4. **2,000 at most** (proposed), for a phone's sake; most recent first.
5. **In the Filter panel** (proposed), since it changes what the map shows, beside the conditions that hide stations.
