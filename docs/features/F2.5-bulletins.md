# F2.5 Bulletins, announcements and groups

Status: reading built for 0.5.0 ([#193](https://github.com/APRS-NET/AprsMap/issues/193), PR #263) on APRS.NET's
`BulletinBoard` (G10, delivered); sending bulletins on a schedule and ANSRVR groups wait on G34.

## User story

As an operator, I see the bulletins and announcements stations send (a net reminder, a weather warning, a club's news),
newest version only and gone when they're old; I post my own with a sensible repeat schedule; and I join an
announcement group such as a club's or an event's, so its messages reach me and mine reach its members.

## Background: what already exists

- F2.4 Messaging: conversations, Send via, the offline queue, F2.1's transmit rules, the gateway's refusal until F2.9.
- APRS.NET's `BulletinBoard` keeps received bulletins (`BLN0`–`BLN9`), announcements (`BLNA`–`BLNZ`), group bulletins
  (the group after the id, such as `BLN4WX`) and NWS bulletins (`NWS-…`), the newest version of each by sender, kind, id
  and group, and removes one not heard for its expiry (a day by default); it raises `Changed`.
- F2.2 Beacons: schedules and Send via checkmarks.

## Behavior

### Reading

1. A **Bulletins** panel (a dockable panel, F1.0; a tab beside Messages) lists what the board holds, grouped as
   **Bulletins**, **Announcements** and each **group**: sender, id, text, when first and last heard, and where it was
   heard (F1.25 source), with **NWS bulletins** in their own section. Newest first; a replaced bulletin shows its new
   text and "updated".
2. New items are marked until the panel is seen; the Messages tab's count doesn't include them (decision 1).
3. An item not heard again for a day goes, as the board does (decision 2); a bulletin its sender keeps repeating stays.
4. **Filters**: by kind and group; text search. A group can be **muted**, hiding it.

### Posting

5. **New bulletin** (operator, licensed, F2.1's rules): kind (bulletin with a digit, announcement with a letter, or a
   group), the id, the text (up to 67 characters, the same checks as a message), and **Send via** checkmarks as for
   beacons (F2.2 behavior 2).
6. A bulletin is **repeated** on APRS.NET's bulletin schedule (G34): often at first, then less often, for as long as the
   operator keeps it (4 hours by default for a bulletin, 2 days for an announcement), then it stops. **Edit** changes
   the text and starts the schedule again; **Stop** ends it. Our own bulletins show in the list marked as ours, with
   their next send.
7. Posting needs what any send needs: a licensed profile, a TX enabled source, the Transmit switch on; on a gateway, a
   signed-in operator (F2.9).

### Groups (ANSRVR)

8. **Join a group** (a name such as `NZEVENT`): sends ANSRVR's join message, through F2.4's messaging (acked and
   retried like any message). The group then has a **conversation** in Messages, holding what ANSRVR relays from the
   group and what I post to it.
9. **Post to a group** from its conversation: sent to ANSRVR in its post form. **Leave** sends the leave message.
10. Joined groups are kept per profile and **kept alive**: ANSRVR drops a member it hasn't heard from, so the app sends
    the keep-alive ANSRVR asks for, once a day while joined (decision 3).
11. Which message forms ANSRVR takes and how its relayed messages are told apart from others is APRS.NET's (G34); the app
    only shows the result.

## Service surface

- `WatchBulletinsAsync()` → `IAsyncEnumerable<BulletinView>` (kind, sender, id, group, text, first and last heard,
  source, mine, next send); the board's current items first.
- `PostBulletinAsync(BulletinPost)` and `StopBulletinAsync(id)`; `BulletinPost` has kind, id, group, text, Send via, how
  long.
- `JoinGroupAsync(name)`, `LeaveGroupAsync(name)`; groups are conversations in `WatchMessagesAsync` with a `Group` field.
- Settings: muted groups per viewer (preference store); joined groups per profile.
- Error codes: `bulletin.text-invalid`, `bulletin.id-invalid`, and F2.1's `transmit.*`.

## APRS.NET dependencies

- **G10** (delivered): `BulletinBoard` for reading.
- **G34** (to file): a bulletin repeat schedule (when to send a bulletin again, for how long), and ANSRVR helpers
  (join, leave, post and keep-alive messages; recognising a message relayed from a group).

## Desktop vs web differences

| | Desktop | Web (gateway) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reading | Everyone | Every viewer |
| Posting, joining, leaving | The user (licensed) | A signed-in operator (F2.9) |

## Acceptance tests

1. A corpus of bulletins, announcements, a group bulletin and an NWS bulletin shows each once, the newest version, in
   its section; one not heard for a day goes.
2. A posted bulletin goes out on the sources ticked, repeats on the schedule, and stops after its time or on Stop.
3. Joining sends ANSRVR's join message through messaging; a relayed group message lands in the group's conversation;
   leaving sends the leave message; the keep-alive goes once a day while joined.
4. Posting without a licence, with Transmit off or from a gateway viewer is refused with the usual reasons.

## Decisions for review

1. **Bulletins aren't unread messages** (proposed): they're broadcast, so they get their own "new" marks.
2. **Expiry from APRS.NET's board** (proposed): gone when not heard for a day, rather than a rule of the app's own.
3. **A daily keep-alive** (proposed) while a group is joined.
4. **Bulletins in their own panel** (proposed), beside Messages, rather than inside it.
