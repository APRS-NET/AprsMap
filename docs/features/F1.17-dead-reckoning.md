# F1.17 Dead reckoning

Status: draft

## User story

As an operator following moving stations, I see where each one probably is now, not only where it was at its last
beacon: a vehicle that beaconed two minutes ago at 90 km/h is shown about 3 km further on, clearly marked as an
estimate, with an indication of how old the real fix is.

## Behavior

1. A station whose last position report carried **course and speed**, with a speed of at least 5 km/h, gets an
   **estimated position**: from the last reported position along the course at that speed, for the time since.
2. Estimates stop after **10 minutes** or when the station goes stale (F1.3), whichever is first; after that the station
   is shown only at its last reported position.
3. **On the map**: the station's symbol moves to the estimated position, drawn with a dashed outline, and a thin dashed
   line joins it to the last reported position, which keeps a small dot. A **freshness ring** around the symbol shows
   the share of the 10 minutes left, shrinking as the fix ages.
4. The station's details (F1.6) show both: "Reported 3 min ago at …" and "Estimated now at …, 4.5 km on".
5. Distance and bearing (F1.13) and list sorting (F1.7) use the reported position, never the estimate.
6. **Dead reckoning** (Settings › Map, and a layer toggle): On · Off; default Off. Per viewer.
7. Estimates move smoothly: they're recomputed each frame from the time (no engine traffic), within F1.4's frame
   budget.

## Service surface

- `StationState` (the map stream) gains `Course` and `Speed` (km/h) and `PositionTime` (when the reported position was
  received), for stations that report them; the UI computes the estimate per frame.
- The estimate needs a destination-point calculation (G08) in the UI, which references only `AprsMap.Contracts`. Until
  that is settled (decision 3), it is a small pure function in the UI, marked `// SHIM: G08`.

## APRS.NET dependencies

- **G08, destination point** from a bearing and distance. **G11**'s dead-reckoning extrapolation covers our own beacons
  (F2.3); for other stations the plain destination point is enough.

## Desktop vs web differences

None.

## Acceptance tests

1. A station at 90 km/h on course 090 reported 2 minutes ago is estimated 3 km east (within 1 %).
2. No estimate below 5 km/h, after 10 minutes, or after the station is stale.
3. The freshness ring shrinks over 10 minutes; dashed symbol and line draw in every palette.
4. Distance, bearing and sorting use the reported position.
5. Performance: 2,000 moving stations with estimates hold the frame budget.

## Decisions for review

1. **Off by default** (proposed): an estimate is a guess, and a viewer should choose to see guesses.
2. **10-minute limit and 5 km/h floor** (proposed).
3. **Where the UI gets the destination-point math**: the UI may reference only `AprsMap.Contracts`, so either a pure
   copy in the UI (`// SHIM: G08`) or the engine sending estimates (heavier streams). Proposed: the copy in the UI,
   replaced when APRS.NET's geodesy is usable from the UI (`AprsNet.*` packages are MIT and could be referenced by
   Contracts if the maintainer agrees).
