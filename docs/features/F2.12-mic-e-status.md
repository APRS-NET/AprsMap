# F2.12 Mic-E status

Status: built for 0.6.0 ([#204](https://github.com/APRS-NET/AprsMap/issues/204)); decisions proposed 2026-10-19 for the
maintainer's review; sending Emergency left out by the maintainer's decision (2026-10-19). In the UI: Settings ›
Beacons › Position › Format.

## User story

As an operator, I send my position as Mic-E with a status (En route, In service, …), the way a mobile radio does.
Received statuses I can already filter on, and emergencies from other stations always show.

## Background: what already exists

- The position beacon (F2.2) is sent uncompressed or compressed, with optional PHG; Send position now sends the same.
- Received Mic-E statuses are a station filter (F1.9 behavior 1.7), and Mic-E emergencies are always shown, whatever the
  filter (F1.9 behavior 3).
- APRS.NET builds Mic-E reports (G01, delivered): the latitude and the status travel in the destination callsign.
- Observed in APRSIS32 (docs/reference/observation-2026-10-13.md): a Mic-E status to send, and per-code
  notifications with alert styles. This design is the app's own.

## Behavior

### Sending

1. **Format**: the position beacon has three formats in Settings › Beacons: **Standard**, **Compressed** and **Mic-E**
   (proposed: Mic-E is a format, not a separate beacon). Standard is the default, as now.
2. **The status**: with Mic-E, a status is chosen: Off duty, En route, In service, Returning, Committed, Special or
   Priority. **Off duty** is the default (proposed), as most trackers send. The seven custom codes are not offered
   (proposed): they have no agreed meaning to other stations.
3. A Mic-E position carries no PHG and isn't compressed (APRS101 chapter 10); those choices are off while Mic-E is
   chosen. The station's comment travels as the Mic-E status text. A fixed station sends no course, speed or altitude.
4. A Mic-E packet's destination holds the latitude, so it doesn't carry the app's TOCALL; the app has no Mic-E device
   suffix (proposed: none until one is registered).
5. Send position now sends the same as the beacon (F2.2 behavior 2).
6. Changing the format asks for the position to be confirmed again, as changing compression does (F2.2 behavior 12);
   changing only the status doesn't (proposed), so a status can change on the road without stopping the beacon.

### Emergency

7. **The app never sends Emergency** (maintainer's decision, 2026-10-19): it isn't among the statuses, the settings
   can't hold it, and nothing in the app turns it on. An operator in an emergency uses their radio. This is left
   unimplemented on purpose, not for later.

### Receiving

8. Filtering by status and always showing emergencies are F1.9's (unchanged). Alert styles for received statuses
    (sound, a pop-up) belong with alerts in F4.7 (proposed: later, not here).

## Service surface

- `MicEStatus` (`OffDuty`, `EnRoute`, `InService`, `Returning`, `Committed`, `Special`, `Priority`); no `Emergency`.
- `PositionBeaconSettings.MicE` (`MicEStatus?`): null for Standard or Compressed; a status for Mic-E.
- Error code: `settings.profile.mic-e-invalid` (Mic-E with compression or PHG).
- No new `IEngineService` member: the setting is saved with the profile.

## APRS.NET dependencies

G01 (Mic-E builder, delivered in `0.1.0-alpha.14`).

## Acceptance tests

1. Mic-E with En route sends a Mic-E packet whose status reads back as En route, at the profile's position, with the
   comment as its text, on every source the beacon goes on.
2. Send position now sends the Mic-E packet too.
3. Mic-E with compression or PHG is refused.
4. Changing the status alone keeps the position confirmed; changing the format doesn't.
5. Emergency isn't offered, and there is no status that sends it.

## Decisions for review

All proposed (2026-10-19), built as described so they can be tried:

1. **Mic-E is a third position format**, with the status beside it, in Settings › Beacons.
2. **Off duty by default**; the seven standard statuses; **no custom codes**.
3. **Never sending Emergency** is decided (maintainer, 2026-10-19), not proposed.
4. **A status change alone doesn't need the position confirmed again**; a format change does.
5. **No Mic-E device suffix** for the app until one is registered.
6. **Received-status alert styles wait for F4.7**; filtering and emergencies-always-shown stay as F1.9 built them.
