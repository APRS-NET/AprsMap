# F1.42 APRS-IS feed and connection

Status: scoped (maintainer request, 2026-11-17); decisions 1–5 by the maintainer.

## User story

As an operator running an IGate, I want to watch only what my own radio hears, without the internet's packets on the
map, and still gate what I hear to APRS-IS and messages from APRS-IS to RF. Today the APRS-IS switch in the status bar
connects and disconnects APRS-IS, so turning off the internet's traffic also stops the IGate. The connection and the
feed are two separate things: whether the station is connected to APRS-IS, and whether what APRS-IS sends is tracked
and plotted.

## Background: what already exists

- The quick toggles (F1.15): **Internet** (the master switch), **APRS-IS** (connect or not; also the sliding switch in
  the status bar's APRS-IS pill and on the dashboard's Connection tile) and **Map tiles**, saved as
  `AppSettings.Toggles` (`Internet`, `AprsIs`, `Tiles`). `ConnectsAprsIs` is `Internet && AprsIs`.
- The IGate (F3.4, both directions) needs the APRS-IS connection, a verified login and Transmit on; with APRS-IS off it
  holds (`transmit.source-off`).
- The automatic filter (F1.2) subscribes to the area around the station and the map views.
- `Engine:AprsIsOnStart` (`aprs_is_on_start` in `gateway.toml`, #415): false turns the APRS-IS switch off as the engine
  starts.

## Behavior

1. **Two switches.**
   1. **APRS-IS connection** (the toggle `AprsIs`, as stored today): connect to APRS-IS or not. It's in the toggles menu
      beside Internet, and on the APRS-IS row of the dashboard's Connection tile (maintainer, decision 3). Off
      disconnects; the IGate holds, messages over APRS-IS stop. With Internet off it's shown off and disabled, as now.
   2. **APRS-IS feed** (the new toggle `AprsIsFeed`): track and plot what APRS-IS sends, or not. It's the sliding switch
      in the status bar's APRS-IS pill, which now reads "APRS-IS feed" / "APRS-IS feed off". With the connection off
      it's shown off and disabled with "APRS-IS isn't connected".
2. **With the feed off and the connection on** (maintainer, decision 1), packets from APRS-IS still reach:
   - the IGate, both ways (F3.4): gating RF to APRS-IS doesn't use the feed, and messages from APRS-IS for stations
     heard on RF are still gated to RF;
   - messages and acks to and from this station (F2.4);
   - the heard-back checks for what this station sent (F2.1 follow-up);
   - query answers (F2.8).

   They are **not** plotted, listed in Packets, counted (status bar, dashboard, statistics), kept in the history, or
   applied to Stations. Packets heard on RF are unaffected.
3. **The filter follows the feed** (maintainer, decision 2): with the feed off, the area filter isn't sent; the filter is
   only `b/<own callsign>`, for the heard-back checks. The APRS-IS servers pass messages for the logged-in station and
   for stations it has gated on the filtered port without any filter (aprs-is.net, server-side filter commands), so
   messaging and the IGate need nothing more. Turning the feed back on sends the full filter again, without a new login.
4. **Saved like the other toggles**, and kept by saves from the app (F1.15 behavior 3). A station saved before this
   feature, with the APRS-IS switch off (disconnected), stays quiet: connection off and feed off (maintainer, decision
   4). One saved with it on has both on.
5. **`aprs_is_on_start` is deprecated** (maintainer, decision 5): ignored; when it's set to false, the gateway logs a
   warning at start saying it no longer does anything and to remove it. The switches' saved state is what counts.
6. **On a gateway**, both switches are operator-only, as the other toggles are (F1.15 behavior 6); viewers see them
   read-only.

## Service surface

- `StationToggles.AprsIsFeed` (`bool?`, null for a station saved before: it then follows `AprsIs`) and
  `StationToggles.ShowsAprsIs` (`ConnectsAprsIs && (AprsIsFeed ?? AprsIs)`).
- `Toggle.AprsIsFeed`, for `SetToggleAsync`.
- `EngineOptions.AprsIsOnStart` stays readable for the warning only.

## APRS.NET dependencies

None.

## Desktop vs web differences

None: both switches are the station's.

## Acceptance tests

1. With the connection on and the feed off, a packet from APRS-IS isn't listed, counted or applied to Stations; a
   packet heard on RF is.
2. With the feed off, a message from APRS-IS to this station arrives, and the IGate still gates RF to APRS-IS and a
   message for a station heard on RF to RF.
3. With the feed off, the filter sent is `b/<own callsign>`; turning it on sends the area filter again.
4. Settings saved before this feature with APRS-IS off load with the connection and the feed off; with it on, both on.
5. `Engine:AprsIsOnStart` false no longer turns anything off, and logs a deprecation warning.
6. The pill's switch flips the feed, not the connection; the menu's APRS-IS connection item flips the connection; with
   the connection off, the pill's switch is disabled.

## Decisions for review

1. **What still uses APRS-IS with the feed off**: IGate both ways, messages, heard-back, query answers; nothing plotted,
   listed or counted (maintainer, 2026-11-17).
2. **No area filter with the feed off**, to save bandwidth (maintainer, 2026-11-17).
3. **The connection switch lives in the toggles menu** beside Internet and on the dashboard's Connection tile; the pill
   is the feed (maintainer, 2026-11-17).
4. **A station saved with APRS-IS off stays quiet**: connection off and feed off (maintainer, 2026-11-17).
5. **`aprs_is_on_start` is deprecated**: ignored, with a warning (maintainer, 2026-11-17).
