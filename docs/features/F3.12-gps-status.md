# F3.12 GPS status

Status: built for 0.6.0 ([#331](https://github.com/APRS-NET/AprsMap/issues/331)); decisions proposed 2026-10-21 for the
maintainer's review. In the UI: the **GPS** panel, opened from the GPS pill's menu or View › Panels.

## User story

As an operator with a GPS, I see what it knows: where it says I am, how sure it is, which satellites it can see and
uses, and the raw lines it sends, so I can tell a good fix from a poor one and find out why a GPS isn't working.

## Background: what already exists

- GPS sources (F3.6): NMEA over serial or TCP, or gpsd; the fix in `EngineStatus.Gps`, the pill says 2D or 3D and the
  satellites used; NMEA lines go to the Source console (F1.28).
- APRS.NET 0.5.0-alpha.2 (G17, G30): `NmeaFixTracker.Quality` and `Satellites` from GGA, GSA, GSV and GST, and
  `GpsdConnection.Quality` and `Satellites` from gpsd's SKY and TPV reports, as `GpsQuality` (satellites used and in
  view, PDOP, HDOP, VDOP, TDOP, GDOP, estimated errors in metres) and `GpsSatellite` (system, ID, signal, elevation,
  azimuth, SNR, used). `GpsdConnection.LineRead` gives gpsd's raw JSON lines.

## Behavior

1. **The GPS panel** shows the active profile's GPS. Without one it says so, and how to add one (Settings › Sources).
   It's a panel like the others (F1.0), in the right dock by default (proposed), opened from the GPS pill's menu
   (**GPS status**) or View › Panels.
2. **The fix**: 2D, 3D or no fix; the position; the altitude with a 3D fix; speed and course in the viewer's units
   (F0.6); and how long ago it came. A fix that isn't fresh (F3.6, 60 s) says so.
3. **Accuracy**: the estimated horizontal and vertical error in metres where the GPS gives them (gpsd always; NMEA
   with GST), as ±. Otherwise, and as well, the dilutions of precision (PDOP, HDOP, VDOP) and the satellites used of
   those in view. A hint says what's good (proposed: "lower is better; below 2 is good, above 5 is poor", from
   APRS.NET's documentation).
4. **Sky view**: a polar diagram, north up, the horizon as the outer ring and 30° and 60° elevation as inner rings.
   Each satellite in view is a mark at its azimuth and elevation:
   1. **filled** when the fix uses it, **hollow** when it doesn't;
   2. its **shape** says its system (proposed: circle GPS, square GLONASS, diamond Galileo, triangle BeiDou, cross
      the rest), so it doesn't depend on colour (F0.6, colour-vision safe);
   3. its number beside it.
   A satellite seen on two signals is drawn once, with the stronger signal.
5. **Satellite list**, under the diagram and the accessible form of it: system, number, elevation, azimuth, signal
   (dB-Hz) and whether it's used, strongest first.
6. **Raw stream**: **Open console** opens the Source console on the GPS (F1.28). gpsd's JSON reports now go there too,
   as lines (F3.6 decision 6's "later").
7. It follows the status poll, as the pill does; nothing is kept once the app closes.

## Service surface

- `GpsStatus.Accuracy` (`GpsAccuracy`: satellites used and in view, PDOP, HDOP, VDOP, horizontal and vertical error in
  metres, speed error in knots) and `GpsStatus.Sky` (`GpsSkySatellite`: system, ID, signal, elevation, azimuth, SNR,
  used), with `GnssConstellation`.
- `TrafficKind.GpsdReport`: a line gpsd sent.
- No new `IEngineService` member.

## APRS.NET dependencies

G17 and G30, extended for this feature and delivered in 0.5.0-alpha.2.

## Desktop vs web differences

None: the engine reads the GPS, every viewer sees the same.

## Acceptance tests

1. NMEA with GSV, GSA and GST gives satellites in view with elevation, azimuth, SNR and used, the DOPs and the error.
2. gpsd's SKY and TPV give the same, and gpsd's lines reach the console.
3. The panel shows the fix, the accuracy (± with an error, DOPs without) and the list strongest first; without a GPS it
   says how to add one.
4. The sky view draws used satellites filled and unused hollow at their place, and at 200 % text.

## Decisions for review

All proposed (2026-10-21), built as described so they can be tried:

1. **A panel**, in the right dock, rather than a dialog: it can stay open while driving.
2. **Shapes for systems**, fill for used: no meaning carried by colour alone.
3. **The DOP hint** in words, from APRS.NET's documentation.
4. **Two signals of one satellite drawn once**, the stronger.
5. **gpsd's JSON in the console** as it comes, one report a line.
