# F1.45 APRS-IS uplink apart from Transmit

Status: built (0.9.0); decisions 1–4 by the maintainer (2026-11-19).

Known consequence: a profile without a TNC can no longer turn Transmit on (`transmit.no-source`); it sends with the
uplink. Tests: `AprsIsUplinkTests` (Core), `ToggleTests.TheUplinkIsItsOwnSwitchAndTransmitDoesntMoveIt` (UI),
`GatewayTransmitTests` (operator only).

## User story

As an operator running a receive IGate, I want to send what my radio hears to APRS-IS without keying my radio. Today
the one Transmit switch (F2.1) governs everything the station sends, on RF and on APRS-IS alike, so an IGate with
Transmit off gates nothing (F3.4 behavior 3), and turning Transmit on to gate also lets beacons and messages go out on
the TNCs. Transmit is about RF; sending to APRS-IS is a separate permission.

## Background: what already exists

- **Transmit** (F2.1 behaviors 4–8): a quick toggle (F1.15), off for every new and existing station, checked when it's
  turned on (a licensed profile, a source that can send), saved, and turned off by saving settings that change what's
  sent. Every send checks it first (`transmit.off`), then the profile, then the source (`TransmitBlock`,
  `SourceBlock`).
- **APRS-IS connection and feed** (F1.44): the connection (`Toggles.AprsIs`) and the feed (`Toggles.AprsIsFeed`),
  separate, in the Station menu, the APRS-IS pill's menu and the dashboard's Connection tile.
- What goes out on APRS-IS today: own beacons, position by hand, objects and items, messages, acks and auto-replies,
  bulletins and group posts, query answers, pings' pongs, and the IGate's uploads (F3.4). What goes out on a TNC: the
  same own traffic, digipeats (F3.5) and the IGate's gating to RF (F3.4 two-way).

## Decisions (maintainer, 2026-11-19)

1. **Two switches.** **Transmit** is RF: everything the station sends on its TNCs (own traffic, digipeats, gating to
   RF). **APRS-IS uplink** is everything the station sends to APRS-IS: own beacons and messages and everything else
   above, and the IGate's uploads. Neither affects the other.
2. **Where:** the uplink switch is in the Station menu (toggles) and the APRS-IS pill's menu, next to the APRS-IS
   connection and feed. Transmit stays the status bar's switch and the Station menu's.
3. **Existing stations** start with the uplink switch as their Transmit switch was, so nothing changes until the
   operator changes it.
4. **The same rules as RF:** a licensed profile (not N0CALL, not a listener id, not receive only) and a verified
   APRS-IS login, both checked when it's turned on and on every send.

## Behavior

1. `StationToggles.AprsIsUplink` (`bool?`), saved with the other toggles. Null, for a station saved before it, means
   "as Transmit" (`UplinkOn => AprsIsUplink ?? Transmit`, decision 3); toggling Transmit or the uplink from then on
   saves both, so the uplink no longer follows Transmit.
2. A send on a source checks that source's switch first: a TNC, Transmit (`transmit.off`, "Transmit is off."); APRS-IS,
   the uplink (`transmit.uplink-off`, "APRS-IS uplink is off. Turn it on in the APRS-IS menu."). Then the profile and
   the source, as before (F2.1 behavior 5). A send to "any source" (Send position now, a message's first try, beacons,
   bulletins) goes on each source whose switch is on and that can send; it's refused only when none can, with
   `transmit.off` when both switches are off.
3. Turning the uplink on is checked like Transmit (F2.1 behavior 5, decision 4): the profile may transmit and the
   APRS-IS source can send (connected, verified, not RX only); otherwise refused with the reason. Off always works. On
   a gateway, turning it on needs a signed-in operator (F2.1 behavior 9).
4. Saving settings that change what's sent, or switching profiles, turns the uplink off with Transmit (F2.1 behavior
   6), for the same reason: the operator confirms what goes out under the new settings.
5. The IGate's uploads (F3.4) are held by the uplink, not Transmit: with the uplink on, the profile's IGate on, a
   licensed profile and a verified login, what the TNCs hear goes to APRS-IS with Transmit off. Gating to RF (F3.4
   two-way) stays under Transmit, and also needs the APRS-IS connection.
6. Status (`TransmitStatus`): `On` and `Blocked` are Transmit's, over the TNCs only; `UplinkOn` and `UplinkBlocked`
   (why APRS-IS couldn't send now, the switch aside) are the uplink's. The Transmit pill says held only for its own
   reasons.
7. The Station menu and the APRS-IS pill's menu have **APRS-IS uplink** as a check item after **APRS-IS feed**,
   disabled with the reason while the Internet or the APRS-IS connection is off, for viewers, or while the station is
   receive only. Send position on APRS-IS (F2.1 behavior 10) needs the uplink, not Transmit.

## Acceptance

1. A station saved with Transmit on loads with the uplink on; one with Transmit off, with it off.
2. With Transmit off and the uplink on, an IGate gates what its TNC hears to APRS-IS, and nothing goes out on the TNC.
3. With Transmit on and the uplink off, a beacon goes out on the TNC and not on APRS-IS; a message to "any source"
   goes on the TNC.
4. With both off, a send is refused with `transmit.off`; with only the uplink off, a send on APRS-IS alone is refused
   with `transmit.uplink-off`.
5. Turning the uplink on with an unlicensed profile is refused with `transmit.not-licensed`.
