# F2.4 Messaging

Status: built for 0.5.0 ([#192](https://github.com/APRS-NET/AprsMap/issues/192)), on APRS.NET's
`AprsNet.Messaging` (G10, in `0.4.0-alpha.2`). The Messages panel docks beside Details; Send message is in a station's
details; unread messages show in the status bar. Revised 2026-10-18 (found in use on an RF-only station, [#297](https://github.com/APRS-NET/AprsMap/issues/297)):
Automatic sends on the TNCs when no APRS-IS source can send, deleted sources leave Send via, and a waiting message says
why (behaviors 8 and 10).

## User story

As a licensed operator, I chat with other stations: one conversation per station, each message showing whether it was
delivered, sent over the way that reaches them (RF to a station my TNC hears, the internet otherwise, or the way I
choose), and messages to me acknowledged and shown once, even when they arrive on several sources.

## Background: what already exists

- F2.1: who may transmit (Licensed, RX only per source, the Transmit switch), every send listed as `Sent`
  with its source, and the gateway refusing transmit calls until F2.9.
- F1.25 and F3.1: packets from several sources at once, each with its source and, for a TNC, heard here directly or
  through digipeaters.
- APRS.NET's `MessagingStation` does the protocol: message IDs, one message in flight per station, retries on a
  `RetryProfile` (Default, APRS-IS, Slow HF, None) that never resends inside APRS-IS's 30-second duplicate window, acks,
  rejs and APRS 1.1 reply-acks, each received message raised once, retry when a failed station is heard again, and the
  source each station was heard on. It does no I/O.

## Behavior

### Conversations

1. A **Messages** panel (a dockable panel, F1.0) lists **conversations**, one per correspondent (callsign with SSID),
   newest activity first, each with its unread count and last line. Opening one shows its messages, oldest at the top,
   with a compose box at the bottom.
2. **Messages to me** are those addressed to the active profile's callsign, exactly (with SSID). Messages to the
   profile's other SSIDs, aliases (other calls treated as me) and listening in on other stations' messages come later.
3. A conversation starts from **New message** (type a callsign), from a station's details (**Send message**), or when
   a station messages me.
4. **Unread**: a received message is unread until its conversation is open and the window has focus. The Messages
   tab, and the status bar while the panel is hidden, show the total ("2 new messages"); screen readers hear each
   new message's sender.
5. Conversations and their messages are kept **per profile** across restarts: the last 500 messages per conversation,
   for 90 days, with the last message ID used, so IDs don't repeat after a restart.

### Sending

6. The compose box takes up to **300 characters** and sends them as messages of at most **67 characters** (APRS101's
   limit), split between words where it can, in order, each its own message with its own ID. A counter says how many
   messages it will make. Characters that can't be sent (`|`, `~`, `{` and anything outside printable ASCII) are
   refused with the reason; nothing is changed silently.
7. Each sent message shows its **state**, in words and a symbol: Queued (waiting for an earlier one, or for a source to
   be able to send), Sent, Retrying (attempt *n* of *m*, next in *t*), Delivered, Rejected, Failed. A queued or
   retrying message can be **cancelled**; a failed one can be **sent again**.
8. **Send via**, per conversation:
   1. **Automatic** (the default): over RF on the TNC that heard the station in the last 30 minutes, when that TNC can
      send; otherwise over APRS-IS. If every RF attempt goes unacked, it's sent once more over APRS-IS, for when no
      IGate hears this station. **With no APRS-IS source that can send** (an RF-only station), it goes on every TNC
      that can send, with its TX path, so a station not heard lately can still be reached through the digipeaters.
   2. **Chosen sources**: checkmarks for each of the profile's sources that isn't RX only; a message goes out on each
      one ticked (receivers drop the repeats by message ID). **Deleting a source takes it out of every conversation's
      Send via**; a conversation left with none goes back to Automatic, and so does one whose chosen sources are all
      missing from the settings when they load.
   The conversation header says which way the next message goes ("via Direwolf, heard 4 min ago"), or, when nothing
   can send, **why**, in F2.1 behavior 7a's words for each source tried: "Waiting: 10.10.20.144 is switched off. Turn
   it on in the status bar."
9. **Retries**, per conversation: Automatic (the APRS-IS profile over APRS-IS, the Default profile over RF), or one of
   Default, APRS-IS, Slow HF and None, named for what they're for.
10. **The offline queue**: while nothing can send (Transmit off, sources off or down), messages wait as Queued, with the
    reason (behavior 8), and go out, in
    order, when a source can send; a message queued for more than 24 hours fails.
11. Sending follows every F2.1 layer; a message can't be written in a conversation of a profile that may not transmit,
    and the compose box says why ("This profile only listens").

### Receiving

12. Messages to me are taken from every source. Each is shown once (APRS.NET's duplicate handling across sources), with
    the source it came on and, for RF, whether it was heard directly.
13. **Acks** go back on the source the message came on, from the profile's callsign, while the profile may transmit
    and that source can send. While it can't (Transmit off, a listening profile), the message is still shown, marked
    "not acknowledged: transmitting is off", and the sender's station keeps retrying.
14. A message that can't be handled (none in this version) would be rejected; nothing is rejected automatically.

### The station says it takes messages

15. Positions this station sends (F2.1, F2.2) say it can receive messages (`=`, not `!`) while the profile may
    transmit, since it now answers them.

### On a gateway

16. Conversations are the station operator's: until F2.9's sign-in, browsers can neither read nor send them
    (`gateway.operator-only`); the Messages panel says so.

## Service surface

- `IEngineService`:
  - `GetConversationsAsync()` → `Conversation` list (correspondent, unread count, last message, Send via, Retries);
  - `WatchMessagesAsync()` → a stream of `MessageEvent` (a message added or its state changed, a conversation's unread
    count changed), starting with the active profile's recent messages;
  - `SendMessageAsync(to, text)` → the `ChatMessage`s it made;
  - `CancelMessageAsync(id)`, `ResendMessageAsync(id)`, `MarkReadAsync(correspondent)`;
  - `SetConversationAsync(correspondent, sendVia, retries)`.
- `ChatMessage` (id, correspondent, direction, text, time, state, attempts, next try, source, heard directly).
- `EngineStatus` gains `UnreadMessages`.
- Error codes: `messages.text-invalid`, `messages.too-long`, `messages.callsign-invalid`, and the `transmit.*` codes.
- Storage: `messages.json` per profile in the settings folder.

## Acceptance tests

1. A message to the profile's callsign heard on APRS-IS and on the TNC is shown once, acked once, on the source it came
   on first, and counted unread until its conversation is opened.
2. Sending 150 characters makes three messages of at most 67, sent one after another; each shows Sent, then Delivered
   when its ack comes; a reply-ack settles the message it answers.
3. Automatic Send via sends over the TNC to a station it heard 10 minutes ago, over APRS-IS to one it didn't, and once
   more over APRS-IS after the RF attempts fail; with no APRS-IS source that can send, it sends on every TNC that can.
3a. Deleting a source chosen in a conversation's Send via leaves the conversation on its other chosen sources, or on
   Automatic when none is left; settings loaded with a chosen source that no longer exists do the same. With the only
   TNC switched off, the header says "Waiting: <name> is switched off. Turn it on in the status bar."
4. With Transmit off, a sent message waits Queued and goes out when Transmit is turned on; a received one is shown, not
   acked, and marked so.
5. After a restart the conversations come back and the next message ID follows the last one.
6. A message with `|` or a non-ASCII character is refused with `messages.text-invalid`.
7. On a gateway, every messaging call is refused with `gateway.operator-only`.
8. Positions are sent with `=` while the profile may transmit.

## Decisions for review

1. **Exact callsign only** (proposed): messages to the profile's callsign with its SSID; other SSIDs, aliases and
   listening in come later.
2. **Automatic Send via falls back to APRS-IS once** (proposed), after the RF attempts go unacked; with no APRS-IS that
   can send, it uses every TNC that can (maintainer, 2026-10-18).
3. **Split long text into 67-character messages** (proposed), up to 300 characters, rather than refusing it.
4. **Printable ASCII only** (proposed), refusing what can't be sent rather than changing it.
5. **Keep 500 messages per conversation for 90 days** (proposed).
6. **No acks while transmitting is off** (proposed): the sender keeps retrying, and the message is marked so.
7. **Messages are operator-only on a gateway** (proposed), even to read.
