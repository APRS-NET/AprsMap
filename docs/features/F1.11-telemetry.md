# F1.11 Telemetry

Status: building ([#125](https://github.com/APRS-NET/AprsMap/issues/125)). Built: the last 100 reports (none older
than 24 hours) per station, from telemetry packets and telemetry in position comments, raw (behavior 3); the details'
Telemetry section with the latest report, a chart per analog channel whose accessible name lists its values, and the
bits (behavior 4); "send telemetry" in the View filter (5); Clear's telemetry (6). `GetTelemetryAsync` returns the
samples. Still to build: keeping definitions and applying them (behaviors 1–2), once APRS.NET's `TelemetryProfile`
(G09, [APRS.NET#153](https://github.com/APRS-NET/APRS.NET/issues/153)) ships. Decision 1 as proposed.

## User story

As an operator, I can read a station's telemetry as it means it: named channels with units ("Battery 12.6 V", "Door:
open"), not raw numbers, and see how the values changed recently.

## Behavior

1. The engine keeps, per station, its **telemetry definitions** (the latest `PARM.`, `UNIT.`, `EQNS.` and `BITS.`
   messages, including ones sent from another callsign and addressed to it) and its **last 100 reports** (or 24 hours,
   whichever is less).
2. Each report is shown with the definitions applied (G09): the five analog channels scaled by their equations, named
   and with their unit; the eight bits named, and shown as on or off according to the station's sense.
3. **Without definitions**, channels are shown raw as `A1`…`A5` and `B1`…`B8`, with a note that the station hasn't
   sent definitions. Definitions that arrive later apply to the reports already held.
4. **Details pane** (F1.6): a Telemetry section with the latest values, the sequence number and the time, and a small
   chart per analog channel over the held reports (a line in the accent colour, theme tokens only), with the values
   in a table for screen readers.
5. Stations with telemetry are a view filter condition (F1.9).
6. Clearing telemetry is part of F1.19.

## Service surface

- New `Task<TelemetryView?> GetTelemetryAsync(string name)`: the station's channel names, units, bit names and the
  held reports with values applied; null when the station has none.
- Engine options: `TelemetryMaxReports` (100), `TelemetryMaxAge` (24 h).

## APRS.NET dependencies

- Telemetry decoding (`TelemetryReport`, `TelemetryDefinition`): delivered.
- **G09, definitions applied to reports**: until it ships, only raw values are shown (behavior 3).

## Desktop vs web differences

None.

## Acceptance tests

1. Reports without definitions show raw channels; `PARM`/`UNIT`/`EQNS`/`BITS` then apply to held reports (APRS101 ch.
   13 example).
2. Definitions sent by another callsign, addressed to the station, apply.
3. 101 reports keep 100; reports older than 24 h drop (fake clock).
4. The chart and its table render in every palette and at 200 % text.

## Decisions for review

1. **History**: 100 reports or 24 hours (proposed), enough for a day of a 15-minute telemetry interval.
